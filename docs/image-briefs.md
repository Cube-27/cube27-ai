# Image Briefs — CUBE27 AI

Use this when you want to replace the in-repo generated art with renders from an
external tool (Midjourney, Firefly, Freepik, Recraft, DALL·E, Stable Diffusion,
or a 3D artist). Each brief is written to be pasted directly as a prompt.

The site ships working art today, generated by `node scripts/generate-art.mjs`.
Nothing here is required — this exists so you can swap in something better
without reverse-engineering the design system.

---

## Non-negotiables

These apply to every image on this site. They come from the redesign direction
and are the difference between looking like a product company and looking like
every other AI landing page.

**Never:**

- brains, neurons, synapses, glowing network nodes, circuit boards, robots,
  humanoid figures, hands touching screens
- the generic purple-to-blue "AI gradient"
- literal HUDs, sci-fi dashboards, fake UI, floating holograms
- stock-photo people at laptops
- any text, letterform, logo or watermark baked into the image

**Always:**

- abstract and material: light, fibre, glass, pigment, folded surface
- a deep near-black ground so the form reads as luminous, not neon
- real negative space — at least a third of the frame quiet
- one dominant form, not a full-frame texture
- CUBE27 brand hues only (below)

---

## Palette

Quote these hex values in the prompt. The crimson is the signature — it is the
colour of the `27` in the CUBE27 mark and should be the dominant hue in the
hero.

| Role                | Hex       | Notes                               |
| ------------------- | --------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Crimson (signature) | `#a91d4b` | dominant; lifts to `#f2789f`        |
| Indigo              | `#33348f` | secondary structure                 |
| Blue                | `#0872ba` | secondary structure                 |
| Purple              | `#6d3d80` | accent only                         |
| Green               | `#0b7a3c` | sparing; easily reads off-brand     |
| Ground              | `#080b14` | near-black with a faint blue-violet |

Do not introduce orange, teal, yellow or cyan.

## 1 — Homepage hero

**Where it goes:** `src/assets/images/hero-field.jpg`
**Aspect:** 5:4 · **Minimum:** 1600 × 1280 · **Format:** JPEG or PNG

The image sits inside a rounded frame on the right of the hero, beside the
headline. It is the only large decorative image on the site, so it carries the
brand impression on its own. It must stay legible when cropped to roughly 16:10
on tablet and to a wide letterbox on mobile — so keep the subject centred and
avoid detail in the outer 10% of the frame.

**Prompt:**

> Abstract sculptural composition of thousands of fine parallel filaments of
> light, sweeping across the frame in two broad ribbons that cross and fold over
> one another. The filaments are individually visible — like brushed silk or
> drawn fibre optics — catching light along their length and fading into
> darkness at the edges. Deep crimson `#a91d4b` and lifted rose `#f2789f`
> dominate; indigo `#33348f` and blue `#0872ba` run through the crossing.
> Near-black blue-violet ground `#080b14`. One form dominates, the rest of the
> frame is quiet and dark. Soft depth of field on the receding strands, crisp
> definition on the foreground bundle. Studio lighting, high dynamic range,
> photographic realism, no visible geometry or wireframe. No text, no logos, no
> people, no technology, no circuitry.

**Negative prompt:**

> text, letters, watermark, logo, brain, neurons, network nodes, circuit board,
> robot, android, human figure, hands, screen, dashboard, interface, hologram,
> sci-fi, cyberpunk, neon signage, purple blue AI gradient, teal, orange, cyan,
> busy, cluttered, symmetrical, tiled pattern

## 2 — Social preview card

**Where it goes:** `public/social-preview.jpg`
**Aspect:** 1.91:1 · **Exactly:** 1200 × 630 · **Format:** JPEG

Shown when a page is shared on LinkedIn, Slack or X. The title and description
come from the page metadata, so **no text belongs in this image** — it is purely
the brand impression. It is often rendered small, so it needs a single readable
gesture rather than fine detail.

**Prompt:**

> Wide abstract banner: a single broad ribbon of fine crimson and rose
> filaments sweeping diagonally across a near-black blue-violet field, with a
> cooler indigo bundle passing beneath it. The strands catch light where the two
> ribbons cross. Generous empty darkness in the upper left. Deep crimson
> `#a91d4b`, rose `#f2789f`, indigo `#33348f`, ground `#080b14`. Calm,
> premium, editorial. Photographic, high dynamic range, shallow depth of field.
> No text, no logos, no symbols.

**Negative prompt:** as above.

## 3 — Optional: per-product hero fields

**Not currently used.** Product pages carry an illustration of the product's own
interface instead, which is more useful to a buyer than abstract art. Generate
these only if you decide a product page should open on a hue field behind or
beside its illustration.

**Where they would go:** `src/assets/images/field-<hue>.jpg`
**Aspect:** 16:9 · **Minimum:** 1600 × 900

Same prompt as the hero, with the dominant hue swapped and the composition kept
calmer — these sit behind content, so they need more quiet area and lower
overall contrast:

| Product                   | Dominant hue     | Supporting      |
| ------------------------- | ---------------- | --------------- |
| AI Visibility & Growth    | blue `#0872ba`   | indigo, crimson |
| Commerce Intelligence     | green `#0b7a3c`  | blue, indigo    |
| Supplier Order Operations | indigo `#33348f` | blue, crimson   |
| Bid & Proposal Operations | purple `#6d3d80` | crimson, indigo |

## Dropping a new image in

1. Save the file to the path named in the brief, keeping the filename.
2. If the extension changes, update the import in
   `src/components/sections/Hero.astro`.
3. `pnpm build` — Astro re-derives responsive AVIF and WebP at 560/840/1120/1600.
4. `pnpm verify` and `pnpm lighthouse` — the hero is the LCP element and is held
   to a 2.5s budget, so an oversized source will fail the gate. Keep the source
   under about 1 MB.

The generated art stays available: `node scripts/generate-art.mjs` rewrites both
files from seed. `ART_SEED=<n> node scripts/generate-art.mjs` renders a
different hero composition if you want to sample alternatives — the committed
hero is seed `8823`.
